# GOAL — "LCARS Flight Deck": a cinematic 3D Star Trek dashboard

You are reviewing rendered frames of a web page against this goal. Score how far it is
from the goal and name the biggest gaps. Be a demanding art director. Do NOT rewrite the
page; judge the frames you are given.

## The target look
The opening / establishing computer-interface shot of a MODERN Star Trek series
(2017+: Discovery, Picard, Strange New Worlds) — the glossy, volumetric, HOLOGRAPHIC
evolution of LCARS, not the flat 1990s TNG panels. Imagine the title sequence where the
camera flies through floating translucent command-console displays suspended in deep space.
A frame you could pause from a real show's VFX: composed, lit, intentional, with depth.

## Non-negotiable LCARS DNA (must be unmistakably LCARS)
- The signature "elbow": a thick vertical bar meeting a horizontal bar through a large
  rounded INTERIOR corner (the half-pill sweep). At least one strong elbow framing the layout.
- Stacked "pill" buttons / rounded-end bars in columns down the sides.
- LCARS palette: amber/orange (#FF9900 #FF7700), mauve/lilac (#CC99CC), periwinkle (#9999FF),
  red-orange (#CC6666), sky (#88CCFF), rare gold (#FFCC66). True-black background.
- Condensed, ALL-CAPS, geometric sans labels (Antonio/Oswald feel), numeric codes, clipped words.
- Colored section-header bars; right-aligned numeric readouts.

## Cinematic 3D qualities (the "new show" bar)
- Deep-space backdrop: starfield + faint nebula/volumetric haze + depth fog.
- Panels are translucent glass/holographic, floating in 3D with real perspective & depth;
  edges glow. Parallax between near and far panels.
- Bloom/glow on emissive elements; subtle lens flare on the brightest points.
- An opening camera move (fly-in / push-through, ~2-4s) that assembles the console, then
  settles to a READABLE resting framing with slow idle parallax.
- Holographic shimmer / scanlines / faint chromatic edge; hints of reflection.

## It must be a real dashboard, not just eye-candy
- System/title bar: "GAS CITY · LCARS", stardate/clock.
- Real metrics as LCARS readouts: quality score, velocity (merges/day), milestone M6 progress,
  active agents, backlog, deploy status. Plausible numbers are fine.
- A central focal element (a "viewscreen"): e.g. a radial/holographic chart of roadmap or a
  rotating city-core.
- Status rows, a legend, a footer code. The settled frame must be legible.

## How to score
Give DISTANCE-FROM-GOAL as a 0-100 SCORE where 100 = indistinguishable from a real modern
Star Trek show's hero interface shot, 0 = generic flat web page.
Anchors: flat CSS boxes ~15; a black screen with one shape ~10; recognizable-but-flat LCARS ~45;
LCARS with real 3D depth + bloom + starfield ~70; cinematic, lit, composed, legible ~85+.
Then list, ranked: the TOP 5 GAPS (what most holds it back from the goal) and, for each, a
SPECIFIC, actionable UX/design requirement to add next round. Flag anything unreadable,
broken, empty, or black. End with one line: "SCORE: NN".
